# The Pirate Token – Skript für KI-Video-Tool

Format: 9:16 Hochformat · Länge ca. 2:25 · Sprache: Englisch · Ende: Cliffhanger (Teil 2)

## Stil (in das Stil-/Style-Feld)

```
Blocky 3D cartoon style like a kids' sandbox game: characters with cube heads, rectangular block bodies,
blocky arms and legs, simple painted faces with big expressive eyes. Low-poly colorful town, soft lighting,
smooth cinematic camera moves, a hard cut every 2 seconds. Vertical 9:16. Bright daytime scenes in pastel
colors, chest hall in purple and gold neon, night scenes in dark blue with warm street lamps.
No logos, no brand names.
```

## Figuren (immer gleich beschreiben)

- **LEO** – 13-year-old blocky boy, messy brown hair, orange hoodie with white stripe, dark blue pants, white shoes.
- **MIA** – Leo's little sister, long dark brown hair, pink shirt with white star, light blue pants.
- **MR. VANCE** – rich blocky man, purple suit, white shirt, red tie, gold chain, black top hat with purple band, mustache.
- **THE GIRL** – blocky girl, blonde ponytail, yellow shirt, blue pants.
- **THE STRANGER** – tall, completely black blocky figure, no face.

## Untertitel

Ein Wort pro Untertitel, mittig im unteren Drittel, neon-gelb (#E8FF3A), fette Cartoon-Schrift, dicke schwarze Kontur.

## Stimme

Young energetic male narrator (16–20 years), bright voice, fast storytelling, slightly dramatic. Keine Pausen zwischen den Sätzen.
